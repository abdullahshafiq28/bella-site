Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 August 2026

Bella is a personal memory assistant: you tell it things to remember, and it reminds you — out loud, at the right time or place. This page says plainly what data that involves, where it goes, and what never happens to it.

What we store

  • Your account — email address (or Google sign-in identity) and settings such as what "morning" and "evening" mean to you.
  • Your memories — the reminders you save: the sentence you said, the title, the time, and the short spoken line Bella reads to you.
  • Your questions — what you ask Bella in chat, and her answers, so your conversation history works.

What stays on your phone only

  • Where you are, minute to minute. Place reminders are watched by your phone's own operating system. Bella is not tracking you, and your continuous location never reaches us.
  • Spoken audio for reminders — generated on your device.
  • Snoozes, and a full offline copy of your reminders — so Bella works with no signal.

The one time location does leave your phone

When you ask Bella to find shops that sell something — "buy a cake whenever I'm free" — it has to ask a map where those shops are. At that moment, and only then, your phone sends an approximate location to our server, which asks Google Places. The coordinates are deliberately rounded to about a kilometre before they are sent, because a shop search across 30 km does not need to know which street you are on.

That approximate point is never written to our database. It is held in the server's memory for up to a day, together with what was searched for, so that the next person asking the same question about the same neighbourhood does not cost another lookup. It is not linked to your account, and it disappears when the server restarts.

Everything after that is on your phone: which shops became reminders, when you pass them, and whether you were free at the time.

Third parties we rely on

  • Supabase hosts the database and sign-in. Your data is protected by row-level security: each account can only ever read its own rows.
  • Google Gemini turns your sentences into structured reminders and transcribes voice notes. Recordings are sent for transcription and are not used by us for anything else.
  • Google Places answers "which shops sell this, near here", using the approximate location described above. It is asked by our server, never by your phone directly.
  • OpenStreetMap answers the same question when Google is unavailable or not configured. It receives the same approximate area and nothing that identifies you.

What never happens

  • Your data is never sold and never shared with advertisers.
  • There is no ad tracking and no analytics that identify you.
  • Nobody at Bella reads your memories except if you ask us to while debugging a problem for you.

Your controls

  • Export everything — Settings → Your data gives you a complete file of everything we hold.
  • Delete everything — deleting your account removes your account and every memory, reminder and chat, permanently.
  • Deleted reminders sit in Trash for 30 days, then are gone for good.

Step-by-step instructions, and what to do if you cannot open the app, are on the account deletion page.

Backups

Encrypted backups of the database are kept for 30 days so a technical failure cannot destroy your reminders. They are protected by the same access rules and expire automatically.
