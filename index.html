What Bella does

🎙 Plain words in

Type or speak naturally — "call Ali next Friday at 6", "pay rent on the 1st every month". No forms, no date pickers.

🔊 A voice out

Reminders fire on the exact minute and speak out loud — locked phone, airplane mode, doesn't matter. Snooze from the lock screen.

📍 Places, not just times

Pin a spot on the map and Bella speaks up when you pass nearby — "you're near the cake shop".

🌅 A morning briefing

At your own morning hour she speaks your day. On empty days, silence — an empty briefing is an alarm nobody set.

🧠 Ask your memory

"What am I forgetting?" answered instantly from your own saved words, quoted back so you can check them.

📵 Works offline

Your reminders live on your phone. No signal changes nothing about whether Bella speaks.
