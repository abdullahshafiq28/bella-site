Delete your account and data

Bella — com.mindvista.bella · Last updated: 25 August 2026

You can delete your Bella account, and everything in it, from inside the app. It takes four taps and does not need to go through us.

From inside the app

  1. Open Bella and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the Danger section.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. You are signed out immediately.

If you cannot open the app

Email husnainkhalid626@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, asking us to delete your account. We action it within 30 days, and we will only ever act on a request that comes from the account's own email address.

What gets deleted, and when

DataWhat happens
Your account and sign-inDeleted immediately
Reminders, memories and chatsDeleted immediately — every table is removed with the account
Settings, including your own hoursDeleted immediately
Voice recordingsNothing to delete — a recording is never stored. It is transcribed and discarded in the same request
LocationNothing to delete — place reminders live on your phone, and shop searches are rounded to ~1 km and never written to our database
Anything held on your phoneRemoved when you sign out or uninstall
Encrypted database backupsExpire automatically within 30 days

Deletion is permanent. There is no way to recover an account afterwards, so if you want a copy of your data first, use Settings → Your data → Export everything before you delete.
