Bella support

Bella, your personal memory assistant · Last updated: 27 November 2026

Questions, problems or ideas: email bella.ai@mindvista.io. Tell us what you were doing and roughly when, and we will take it from there.

A reminder arrived but did not speak

Bella speaks through the notification sound. Check that your phone is not on silent, that the volume is up, and that notifications are allowed for Bella in your phone's Settings.

The home screen says “Nothing scheduled yet”

That number counts the reminders armed on this phone. Bella can only arm them once notifications are allowed, so allow notifications for Bella and open it again. Your saved reminders are not lost.

A place reminder did not go off

Place reminders need location access set to Always, so your phone can notice when you arrive even while Bella is closed. Bella asks for it only when you pin a place. You can check it in your phone's Settings, under Bella, then Location.

AI services

Bella uses Google Gemini to understand what you type or say, and Deepgram for her voice. She asks before sending them anything, and you can switch them off any time in Settings → Your data → AI services. Reminders you have already saved keep going off either way.

Credits

Every account gets 500 free credits every 30 days, and a saved reminder uses 2. Nothing is for sale. If they run out, Bella tells you when they refill, and everything already saved keeps working.
